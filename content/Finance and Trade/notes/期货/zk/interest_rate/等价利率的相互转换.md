# 等价利率的相互转换

假设有两个等价的利率 $R_{1}$、$R_{2}$，对应的 compounding frequency 分别为 $m_{1}$、$m_{2}$，则

$$
A \left(1 + \frac{R_{1}}{m_{1}} \right)^{m_{1}n} = A \left(1 + \frac{R_{2}}{m_{2}} \right)^{m_{2}n}
$$
$$
\left(1 + \frac{R_{1}}{m_{1}} \right)^{m_{1}} = \left(1 + \frac{R_{2}}{m_{2}} \right)^{m_{2}}
$$
$$
R_2 = m_2 \left[\left(1 + \frac{R_1}{m_1} \right)^{m_1 / m_2} - 1\right]
$$
